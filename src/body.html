How to hit

TimeA ring closes in on your yellow contact circle as the pitch comes. Tap anywhere (or press Swing) when the ring lands on the circle: it turns green in the perfect moment. AimAim: Auto puts the circle on the pitch for you. Lock-on pulls it toward the pitch. Manual is all you: drag anywhere to move it. Switch with the Aim button while batting. GradesEvery swing gets two grades: Timing (early, perfect, late) and Contact (square, under, over). Perfect and square is always hit hard. SwingsContact (bigger circle and window), Normal, or Power (smaller, harder hit).
